QUESTIONS WE HEAR

Practical questions.
Clear answers.

Start with a specific business result, clear expectations, and realistic implementation steps.

Is this only for businesses that need a brand-new website?

No. Sometimes the right first step is improving an existing site, strengthening its search visibility, clarifying a contact path, or connecting a process you already use.

Is SEO a guaranteed way to rank first?

No. Rankings and traffic depend on many factors, including competition and search engine changes. Good SEO improves technical quality, clarity, relevance, and measurement—but cannot guarantee positions or revenue.

Can you build an app before we have a large customer base?

Yes, provided the app solves a real problem. A small pilot or responsive web app can help validate demand before taking on native app-store requirements or extensive maintenance.

Do SEO and apps have to be purchased together?

No. They are separate starting points. Integration can be considered when it solves a useful, measurable business problem.

How does BOS relate to SEO & Apps?

SEO helps prospects discover a business; apps help customers and teams interact with it. A Business Operating System can coordinate selected next steps—such as handling inquiries or work orders—within defined permissions and human oversight.
